## Design Context

Strategic and visual context live in **PRODUCT.md** (register, audience, brand, anti-references, principles) and **DESIGN.md** (visual tokens, type, components). impeccable commands load these automatically; read them before any design or UI change.

- **Register:** brand. Personal portfolio, the design itself is part of the pitch.
- **Audience:** hiring managers and prospective clients, equally weighted, scanning fast in evaluation mode.
- **Emotional target:** quietly impressive. Personality: Precise. Understated. Honest.
- **Principles:** (1) let the work talk; (2) the site is itself a work sample; (3) honest by default; (4) credible to two rooms in one voice; (5) confidence through understatement.
- **Never:** the generic dev-portfolio template look, or the corporate SaaS landing look.
- **Accessibility bar:** WCAG 2.2 AA, fully met (a brand requirement, not just compliance).
